## Precise Knob 1.1.0 – configurable Anti-EQ

Match Anti-EQ to the equalizer on your radio, speaker or car stereo.

### What's new
- Choose **1–10 bands** instead of the fixed five-band layout.
- Tap a band's frequency to set it anywhere from **20 Hz to 20 kHz**; gains remain adjustable from −12 to +12 dB.
- Presets adapt to your chosen frequencies. Changing the band count redistributes frequencies and interpolates the gains.
- Band layouts and gains are saved automatically. Updating preserves existing five-band settings.
- The chart and correction filters use your configured frequencies.

### Install or update
Download `PreciseKnob-1.1.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Requires Android 9 (API 28) or newer.

This APK uses the same release signing key as 1.0.0, so it can update that version while keeping your settings. Builds signed with a different key, including debug builds, must be uninstalled first, which removes their local data.

### Notes
- Anti-EQ is fitted to the bands available in Android's system equalizer; configurable bands do not increase its physical resolution.
- Fine volume and EQ depend on the global audio effects supported by your device or ROM.
- Validation: 32 unit tests passed and the release APK was built with R8 and resource shrinking. This version has not been tested on a connected phone.
