## Precise Knob 1.0.0 – first stable release

A rotary volume dial with 1 % steps and AutoEq headphone correction.

### Highlights
- 🔊 **Fine volume**: rotary dial with 1 % resolution on top of Android's native steps, configurable detents (1/2/5/10 %) and haptic clicks.
- 🎧 **EQ Correction**: search and download AutoEq profiles (~8,800 headphones) or import `ParametricEQ.txt`, with a live response curve.
- 📻 **Anti-EQ for external equipment**: enter what your radio, speaker or car stereo does to the sound (5 bands, ±12 dB, presets) and the app applies the opposite. The chart shows the device's effect against the correction applied.
- 🔘 **Volume Button Override** *(beta)*: physical volume keys move the volume in fine steps in any app, with a custom popup.
- 🎨 New **steel blue** Material 3 theme (dark, light or system). Headphones and Anti-EQ now live in separate cards.

### Install
Download `PreciseKnob-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Requires Android 9 (API 28) or newer.

### If Android complains
- **Play Protect blocks the install**: tap *More details → Install anyway*.
- **"Restricted setting" for the Volume Button Override** (Android 13+): *Settings → Apps → Precise Knob → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings*, then enable the service in Accessibility. The override screen has a shortcut to the app info.

### Notes
- Fine volume and EQ use Android's global audio effects; some devices or ROMs do not honour them and the app falls back to native steps.
- This build is signed with a new release key. Debug or pre-release builds signed with a different key must be uninstalled first (their data is not kept).
- Tested on a Redmi Note 8 Pro and a Realme RMX3370.
